## Full description (4000 chars max)

KEEP IT UP is a one-click arcade game about a jelly ball that knows exactly one
word: boing. Tap or click near the ball and it flies AWAY from your finger — so
where you tap is how you steer. Keep it off the floor. That is the whole game,
and it will own your thumbs.

THE ONE RULE
• Tap near the ball to bounce it away from your finger.
• Quick taps are weak. Let it fall a beat for a full-power boing, and chain
  bounces inside the combo window to multiply your score (caps at x10).
• A miss only breaks your combo. The floor does not forgive.

THREE MODES
• Classic — the pure loop. Hazards start after a grace period and never spawn
  on top of you; skim one without touching it for a near-miss bonus.
• Climb — the hard one. Score is metres climbed, the camera only ever moves up,
  every wall of saws leaves one dashed corridor, and lava rises forever below.
• Daily run — today's date seeds a remix of gravity, saw pace, saw speed and
  star rate. Everyone gets the same mix; only your best for the day is kept.

WHAT KILLS YOU
Saw — crosses the screen after a red edge flash warns you.
Spike — a ceiling marker telegraphs for a second, then drops where you WERE.
Drone — slow homing, capped turn rate, dies on walls. Out-fly it.
Lava (Climb) — rises forever after its grace period. It is your floor now.

WHAT HELPS YOU
Star — points in Classic, metres in Climb.
Shield — eats exactly one hazard hit, then pops.
Slow-mo — the whole simulation dilates to 45% for a few seconds. Saws included.
Magnet — stars fly to you from across the screen.
Heavy star — a fat pile of points, but gravity +15% for eight seconds.
Greed is a choice.

KEEP COMING BACK
Four achievements, four ball skins unlocked by best score, per-mode bests
remembered on your device, and a copyable share card for every run. Mute, pause
and reduced-motion are one key or tap away.

BUILT DIFFERENT
One self-contained file: no ads, no tracking, no accounts, no purchases, and no
internet permission at all — the game could not phone home if it tried.
Fairness first: every hazard telegraphs and is reachable, every difficulty ramp
has a cap, and item density is normalised per play area so a phone, a tablet
and a monitor all feel the same.

Controls: tap or click to bounce. That's it. Go keep it up.
