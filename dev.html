ONE-CLICK ARCADE

KEEP IT UP

The jelly ball knows only one word: boing. Pick a mode.

the ball forgives misses — never the floor

The one rule
  • Click / tap near the ball — it is pushed away from your finger, so where you click steers it.
  • Quick taps are weak. Let it fall a beat for a full-power boing, and chain bounces inside 2.2s for combo (scoring caps at ×10).
  • A miss only breaks your combo. P / Esc pauses, M mutes.

Classic mode
  • Score = bounce points × combo + pickups. Your best is saved.
  • The floor kills. Hazards start after 9s and never spawn on top of you.
  • Skim a hazard without touching it for a +15 near-miss.

Climb mode — the hard one
  • Score = metres climbed. The camera only ever moves up.
  • Every 300px a wall of saws blocks the sky with one dashed corridor — that is your way through.
  • Lava rises from below after a 3s grace. It is your floor now.
  • Corridor stars pay +5m; every third wall hides a shield.

Daily run

  • Classic rules, but today's date remixes gravity, saw pace, saw speed and star rate.
  • Everyone gets the same mix; only your best for the day is remembered.

Hazards — what kills you
  • Saw — crosses the screen; a red edge flash warns you before it arrives.
  • Spike — a ceiling marker telegraphs for 1s, then it drops where you were. Keep moving.
  • Drone — after 20s. Slow homing, capped turn rate, dies on walls. Out-fly it.
  • Lava (climb) — rises forever after its grace. Touching it ends the run.

Pickups — what helps you
  • Star — +40 points (+5m in climb). Blinks just before it despawns.
  • Shield — eats exactly one hazard hit, then pops.
  • Slow-mo — time crawls at 45% for 4s. Saws included.
  • Magnet — stars within 150px fly to you for 6s.
  • Heavy star — +200 points, but gravity +15% for 8s. Greed is a choice.

Progress

Badges: ×10 combo · 3 stars in one run · survive 120s · a shield save.

Skins unlock at best 0 / 600 / 1800 / 4000. Click one to wear it.
