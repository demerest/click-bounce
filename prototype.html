ONE-CLICK ARCADE

KEEP IT UP

The jelly ball knows only one word: boing. Keep it off the floor.

  • Click / tap near the ball — it gets pushed away from your finger, so you can steer it.
  • Quick taps are weak. Let it fall a beat for a full-power boing — and chain combos.
  • Miss and your combo breaks. The floor and the saws end the run. Stars are points; the teal ring is a one-hit shield.
