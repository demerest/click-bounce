KEEP IT UP

The jelly ball knows only one word: boing. Keep it off the floor.

space / enter also works · M mutes · P pauses

  • Quick taps are weak. Let it fall a beat for a full-power boing — and chain combos (max ×10).
  • Missing breaks your combo, nothing more. The floor and hazards end the run.

  • Skim a saw for a near-miss bonus. P or Esc pauses.
  • Climb mode: race upward for metres while lava rises; follow the dashed corridor through each saw band.
