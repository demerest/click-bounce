KEEP IT UP — PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: 2026-11-05 · applies to every build of the game: the web build (including this site and itch.io) and the Google Play app com.demerest.keepitup.

The short version

KEEP IT UP collects no personal data whatsoever. There is no account system, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no crash reporting and no third-party SDK of any kind. The game is a single self-contained file that makes zero network requests while playing; the Android app does not even declare the INTERNET permission, so it could not phone home if it wanted to.

What the game stores, and where

A few preferences and scores are kept on your device only, in the browser's local storage (web build) or the WebView's equivalent private storage (Android app):

  • your best score per mode (classic, climb) and per daily challenge,
  • unlocked achievements and the ball skin you selected,
  • the mute preference.

This data never leaves your device, is never transmitted anywhere, and is never shared or sold. No cookies are used.

How to delete it

Delete the stored data at any time: in a browser, clear the site's local storage (or all site data) for this domain; on Android, clear the app's data in system settings or uninstall the app. Either way everything the game kept is gone, and nothing else is retained because nothing else ever existed.

Children

The game is a family-friendly arcade title and is not directed at children under 13 in a way that involves data processing — there is no data processing. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, of any age, because the game has no means to collect any.

Third parties

None. No advertisers, no analytics providers, no social plugins, no cloud saves. The stores that distribute the game (GitHub Pages, itch.io, Google Play) apply their own privacy policies to your use of their platforms; this policy covers the game itself.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever gains features that change any of the above (for example an optional online leaderboard), this page will be updated with a new effective date before that version ships, and the store listings will point here.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/demerest/click-bounce/issues or use the contact address shown on the game's store listings.
